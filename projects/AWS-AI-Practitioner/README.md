### 🟪 &nbsp;The road

Seven stages, in the order the official prep plan puts them. The method is measure first, then fill only the gaps the measurement finds.

| | Stage | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Groundwork — the cloud and machine learning vocabulary this exam assumes | **Done** |
| 2 | Official practice question set — learn how the questions are built | **In progress** |
| 3 | Pretest, cold and under exam conditions — find out what I actually do not know | **Next** |
| 4 | The eight learning-plan courses, weighted by whatever the pretest flags | Open |
| 5 | Domain reviews and practices, all five domains | Open |
| 6 | Official practice exam — a readiness check, not a diagnosis | Open |
| 7 | Book the seat and sit the AIF-C01 | Open |

**Stages 3 and 6 look alike and are not.** The pretest diagnoses at the start; the practice exam confirms at the finish. I had them next to each other at the end of the plan until I read the official method properly, which put stage 3 back where it belongs — first.

**Stage 4 is deliberately vague on volume.** The bundle is billed at roughly twenty-three hours for someone starting from nothing. I am not starting from nothing, and the pretest is what decides how much of it I sit through.

### 🟦 &nbsp;A route I dropped

Before this, I spent seven hours on the community programme and passed two courses there, *Cloud 101* and *Machine Learning Foundations*. That route does not reach the certification, and I am not carrying it further.

The hours were not wasted, which is why stage 1 is marked done. *Machine Learning Foundations* is this exam's own subject matter, and the cloud groundwork — storage, compute, functions, access management, networking, the shared responsibility model — is knowledge this exam assumes you arrive with. What I lost was a shortcut, not the learning.

The same applies to the free exam voucher on offer there. It is earned through ninety-day posting and liking streaks rather than through study, so it would arrive after the events I want the certificate for, and I would spend three months starting conversations for a counter instead of an answer. I will pay for the seat.
