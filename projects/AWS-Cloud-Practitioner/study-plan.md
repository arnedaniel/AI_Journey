# Study Plan — AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)

The exam attempt is valid until **30 November 2026**. The plan runs towards that date, next to a full semester, so it is built around something I can do in short sessions rather than long ones.

---

### <a name="leaf-1"><img src="../../assets/leaves/leaf-1.svg" width="16" alt=""></a> &nbsp;What the deck is made of

**Cloud Practitioner Essentials**, all thirteen modules, from the introduction to the cloud to well-architected solutions.<br>
**The four domain reviews**, one per exam domain, with the worked practice questions that come with them.<br>
**The exam guide**, as the checklist: the deck is sorted by its nineteen task statements, and each card names the one it belongs to.

### <a name="leaf-2"><img src="../../assets/leaves/leaf-2.svg" width="16" alt=""></a> &nbsp;The goals

<a name="goals"><img src="../../assets/goals/aws-cloud-practitioner.svg" alt="Goal dates per exam domain, the Discovery Day on 20 November marked with an asterisk, from 16 October to the end of the attempt on 30 November" width="100%"></a>

**Domain 1, Cloud Concepts** — planned for 23 October, done on 1 November<br>
**Domain 2, Security and Compliance** — 9 November<br>
**Domain 3, Technology and Services** — 20 November<br>
**Domain 4, Billing, Pricing, and Support** — 25 November<br>
\* **AWS Discovery Day** — 20 November, 13:30 to 16:30: an afternoon of Cloud Practitioner Essentials, live and virtual with an AWS training partner. One walk through the whole course a week before the exam<br>
**Practice exams** — the official practice set of each domain right after its cards, and on 26 November one last pass over all four. Every gap they show goes back into the cards<br>
**The exam** — 27 November, three days before the attempt runs out

Re-planned on 1 November: domain 1 took longer than planned, so the remaining dates moved back and the pace went up to about fourteen new cards a day, six days a week. The bars fill as cards are learned for the first time. Each practice set comes only after its domain's cards on purpose: it measures what has stuck, so it only means something once every card of that domain has been seen.

### <a name="leaf-3"><img src="../../assets/leaves/leaf-end.svg" width="16" alt=""></a> &nbsp;What is deliberately not in here

**No second pass through the videos.** The deck is the condensed version of them.<br>
**No published deck.** It is built from AWS training material and stays private.<br>
**No hour target.** Cards studied and topics covered are the measure. Since 21 October the time per day is noted in the [progress log](./progress.md), to see the total effort, not to hit a number.
