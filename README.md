### ⬜ &nbsp;How this repository works

Every project lives in its own folder under `projects/`, holding three files: a `README.md` for what it is, a `study-plan.md` for where it is going, and a `progress.md` of dated entries, newest first.

Nothing here is backdated and nothing is tidied up afterwards. The pace in `progress.md` is the real one — about 19 minutes a day against a 30-minute target — not the one I wish I had.
