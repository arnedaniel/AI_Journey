### How this repository works

Every project lives in its own folder under `projects/`, with three files: a `README.md` for what it is, a `study-plan.md` for where it is going, and a `progress.md` of dated entries, newest first.

Nothing is backdated and nothing is tidied up afterwards — the pace in there is the real one, not the one I wish I had. Concepts and resources collect in `notes/`.
